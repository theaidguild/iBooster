## Design Guidelines to Mention

When using these prompts, OpenArt.ai will auto-enhance them, but you can guide the enhancement by including:

- **Descriptive Language**: Use words like "sleek", "elegant contours", "streamlined", "embodies"
- **Color Flow**: Describe gradients as "transitions seamlessly from [color] flowing into [color]"
- **Energy & Motion**: Include phrases like "dynamic energy", "upward motion", "bursting with"
- **Background Compatibility**: Always mention "striking against both light and dark backgrounds"
- **Atmosphere**: End with "The overall atmosphere conveys [desired feeling/concept]"
- **Technical Specifications**: "Modern flat design", "minimalist", "mobile app interface"

## Prompt Variations

You can modify the base prompts by:

- Adding color specifications: "using blue (#007AFF) and green (#34C759) color scheme"
- Specifying style: "in the style of iOS app icons" or "Material Design inspired"
- Adding effects: "with subtle shadow" or "glossy finish" or "matte flat design"
- Platform focus: "optimized for iOS" or "Android adaptive icon style"

## Testing Approach

Generate 3-5 variations of your favorite concepts, then:

1. Test at different sizes (1024px down to 60px)
2. Check visibility on light and dark backgrounds
3. Ensure the design remains clear and recognizable when scaled
4. Consider how it will look with iOS corner rounding and Android adaptive icon treatments

Remember: Keep the design simple and bold enough to work at small sizes while maintaining the professional, tech-focused aesthetic that matches your app's purpose.
